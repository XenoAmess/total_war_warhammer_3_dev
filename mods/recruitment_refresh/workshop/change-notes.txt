0.2.4（包含未单独发布的 0.2.3 修复）
- 点击刷新时，选中的非空普通招募类别不足 3 名候选就补足到 3 名；已有更多候选时不缩减，重复刷新不持续累加。
- 传奇、不朽、受伤、已招募和履历不明人物继续受到保护；补充候选不会绕过原版招募费用、容量或解锁条件。空池不自动生成。
- 新增精确引擎构建校验，已验证 Windows x64 游戏 9.0.4 / 9.0.5；未知构建停止原生刷新。
- 明确禁止商队、车队等特殊招募类型进入普通刷新；这不是特殊招募兼容支持。
- 改善原生候选池校验失败和招募面板关闭失败的错误提示；保留结果不明时停止重试的保护。
- 9.0.5 基斯里夫离线实机通过 1→3、2→3、5→5、保护人物保留、新增候选正常招募和原版按钮避让。不宣称全种族或其他 Mod 兼容。

0.2.4 (includes the previously unpublished 0.2.3 fixes)
- Refresh fills a selected nonempty ordinary subtype pool to at least 3 candidates. Larger pools retain their size; repeated refreshes do not keep adding candidates.
- Protected characters remain. Normal recruitment costs, capacity and unlock requirements still apply. Empty pools are not automatically filled.
- Exact engine-build validation for verified Windows x64 9.0.4 / 9.0.5 builds; unverified builds disable native refresh.
- Caravan and convoy recruitment is explicitly excluded from ordinary refresh.
- Clearer errors for native pool validation and panel closure failures; uncertain operations remain locked against automatic retries.
- Verified offline on Kislev in 9.0.5: 1-to-3, 2-to-3, 5-to-5, protected characters, recruitment of added candidates and native button clearance. Universal faction or other-Mod compatibility is not claimed.
